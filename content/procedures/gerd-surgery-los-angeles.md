## Hiatal Hernia & GERD

** Weak Lower Esophageal Sphincter (LES):** The muscle that controls the opening between esophagus and stomach becomes weakened

** [Hiatal Hernia](/expert-hiatal-hernia-surgery-in-los-angeles/):** When part of the stomach pushes up through the diaphragm

** Lifestyle Factors:** Obesity, smoking, alcohol consumption, and certain medications

** Dietary Triggers:** Spicy foods, citrus fruits, chocolate, coffee, and fatty meals

** Medical Conditions:** Pregnancy, diabetes, and connective tissue disorders

** Eating Habits:** Large meals, eating late at night, or lying down after eating

## Who Is an Ideal Candidate for GERD Surgery?

  * **Chronic GERD (acid reflux/heartburn)** not controlled with lifestyle changes, diet, or medications
  * **Frequent reflux symptoms** [affecting sleep](https://healthylifebariatrics.com/heartburn-gerd-surgery-for-better-sleep/) or quality of life
  * **Ongoing symptoms** despite daily acid-reflux medication
  * **GERD complications** (esophagitis, Barrett’s esophagus, or stricture)
  * **Hiatal hernia** contributing to reflux

## How Long Does Recovery Take After GERD Surgery?

Recovering from **GERD surgery** is usually smooth and straightforward, especially with **minimally invasive techniques**. Most patients return home the same day or within 24 hours. You can expect:

  * **First Few Days** - Mild discomfort or swelling managed with prescribed medications. Light walking is encouraged to promote circulation.

  * **First 1-2 Weeks** - Soft diet recommended while the esophagus heals. Avoid heavy lifting and strenuous activities.

  * **3-4 Weeks** - Gradual return to normal eating habits and light exercises. Most patients feel significantly better by this stage.

  * **Full Recovery (4-6 Weeks)** - Resume normal lifestyle, including physical activities, after your surgeon’s clearance.
