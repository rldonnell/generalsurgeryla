## What Are Colonoscopy and Endoscopy?

Colonoscopy and endoscopy are advanced diagnostic procedures that provide a direct, high-definition view of your digestive tract. Utilizing a thin, flexible camera, these minimally invasive examinations allow [Dr. Moein](/general-surgeon-dr-babak-moein/) to evaluate the internal health of the esophagus, stomach, and colon with exceptional precision, often identifying issues that traditional imaging might miss.

These procedures are essential for proactive health management, serving as the gold standard for colon cancer screening and the diagnosis of chronic gastrointestinal issues. By detecting inflammation, polyps, or structural abnormalities in their earliest stages, we can ensure a more accurate diagnosis and a highly effective treatment plan tailored to your recovery.

### Preventive Screening

Essential for **colon cancer screening** and for evaluating patients with a personal or family history of gastrointestinal disease.

### Upper Digestive Symptoms

Recommended for **chronic heartburn, acid reflux, and difficulty swallowing** to assess the esophagus and stomach.

### Bowel Habit Changes

Helpful for evaluating **chronic diarrhea, persistent constipation, and rectal bleeding**.

### Abdominal Discomfort

Used to investigate **persistent abdominal pain, chronic bloating, and digestive discomfort** that requires direct visualization.

### Warning Indicators

Important in the workup of **iron deficiency anaemia, unexplained weight loss, and ongoing nausea or vomiting**.

### Structural Evaluation

Allows direct assessment of **inflammation, ulcers, polyps, and bleeding sources** to support an accurate diagnosis and targeted treatment.

Most patients can return to **normal light activities by the following day** if no major intervention was required.
