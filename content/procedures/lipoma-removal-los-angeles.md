Lipoma removal surgery is a small surgical procedure used to treat bothersome or cosmetically concerning fatty growths. Lipomas are small, [benign (noncancerous)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Benign_tumor) growths of fat tissue. They usually occur just beneath the skin. Lipomas can occur in any location on the body. They can appear on the arms, shoulders, back, neck, chest, and thighs. Lipomas are often movable, soft, and slow-growing. Even though most lipomas are harmless and painless, some can be bothersome because of their size, location, tenderness, or appearance. If you are experiencing discomfort, appearance, or uncertainty about a growth, a good evaluation is the first step in your procedure.

## Who Is a Good Candidate for Lipoma Removal?

If you have a lump that is growing and causing you discomfort, pressure, restricted movement, and/or cosmetic concerns, then you might be a good candidate for a lipoma removal. Patients also consider removing their lipoma when it is noticeable under clothing, it hurts, or they are concerned about it for unknown reasons. It is best to consult Dr. Moein about your condition to determine if the lump you have is possibly a lipoma and if it should be removed.

## Lipoma Removal Procedure

The removal of a lipoma is usually an easy procedure performed by a skilled surgeon using a precise surgical technique to remove the fatty mass through an incision in the affected area. The method of removal is usually determined by the lipoma's size and location, as well as the condition of the affected area. However, Dr. Moein is dedicated to removing and closing the area to achieve a smooth contour and reduce scarring as much as possible.

## Lipoma Removal Recovery and Aftercare

The recovery period after the removal of the lipoma is usually well-tolerated, and one can return to normal light activities shortly after treatment, depending on the size and location of the removed lipoma. Soreness, swelling, or bruising can occur after the procedure but subsides in the following days.
