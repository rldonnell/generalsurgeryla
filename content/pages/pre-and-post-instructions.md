## Pre-Operative Preparation: Before General Surgery

To ensure your journey to recovery is seamless, your surgery begins long before you enter the operating room. Quality preoperative preparation is central to reducing the risks posed by anesthesia, lowering the risk of postoperative infections, and **shortening the associated recovery time**. Whether you are preparing your body for a routine hernia repair or a more sophisticated anti-reflux procedure, the healing process begins with adhering to the specialized protocols established here at **General Surgery LA**.

### Essential Pre-Op Checklist

  * **Strict Fasting (NPO):** Do not consume any food, water, candy, or gum after midnight on the eve of your surgery. An empty stomach is a critical safety requirement for general anesthesia.
  * **Medication Review:** Certain medications, particularly blood thinners and specific herbal supplements, must be discontinued 7-10 days prior to your appointment.
  * **Skin Preparation:** Showering with antibacterial soap the night before and the morning of surgery significantly lowers the risk of post-operative infection.
  * **Support System:** You must arrange for a responsible adult to drive you home and assist you during the first 24 hours of your immediate post-surgery phase.

### Procedure-Specific Considerations

### GERD & Hiatal Hernia Surgery

For patients undergoing **Laparoscopic Fundoplication** or **Hiatal Hernia Repair**, we may recommend a clear liquid diet 24 hours prior to surgery to ensure the stomach and esophagus are clear. Continue your prescribed acid-reflux medications until the morning of the procedure unless otherwise instructed by our team.

### Gallbladder Removal (Cholecystectomy)

Leading up to your **Gallbladder Surgery**, it is vital to maintain a very low-fat diet. Consuming high-fat meals can trigger a gallbladder attack, which may complicate the timing of your elective procedure or lead to an urgent surgical need.

### Inguinal & Paraesophageal Hernia Repair

If you have a symptomatic bulge, avoid heavy lifting or strenuous exertion even before your surgery date to prevent the risk of an **Incarcerated or Emergency Hernia**. If you notice sudden, severe pain that cannot be pushed back, contact our team immediately for **Emergency Hernia Repair**.

## Your Surgical Journey: Pre-Operative Preparation

At **General Surgery LA**, we believe that a successful surgery begins with thorough preparation. By following these guidelines, you help us ensure the highest safety standards, minimize risks during anesthesia, and set the stage for a **shorter recovery time** and **minimal scarring**.

### Pain & Comfort

During the **Immediate Post-Surgery** phase (Days 1-3), it is normal to experience mild pain, swelling, and soreness. These symptoms are managed with medications prescribed by our team.

### Activity Levels

**Early Recovery** (Weeks 1-2) focuses on light walking. You must strictly avoid heavy lifting or strenuous exertion as your internal tissues begin to heal.

### Work & Routine

In the **Intermediate Recovery** stage (Weeks 3-4), most patients can resume light work. You will notice increased mobility during this time.

### Full Recovery

A **Full Recovery** (Weeks 4-6) marks the milestone for returning to sports. High-impact exercise should only be started after final clearance from **Dr. Moein**.

### Specific Recovery Differences

While general protocols apply to all surgeries, certain procedures have unique recovery nuances as listed below:

Procedure Type | Dietary Focus | Activity Restrictions
---|---|---
**Hernia Repairs** | High-fiber diet to avoid constipation/straining. | Strict no-lifting for 6 weeks to protect the mesh or repair.
**GERD / Reflux Surgery** | Gradual progression from liquid to soft foods for 2-4 weeks. | Focus on avoiding large meals; light activity encouraged.
**Gallbladder Removal** | Low-fat diet recommended for the first 2 weeks. | Standard post-op activity; typically a faster return to daily routines.
